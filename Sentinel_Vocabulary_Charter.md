II. Canonical Sentinel Terms

Below are the official, Founder-approved definitions of all Sentinel-exclusive terms.

1. Table A — The Room of Disagreement

Classification: Narrative IP
Definition:
The conceptual place where two market participants disagree.
One participant is aligned with the underlying causal environment; the other is misaligned.
Sentinel identifies this misalignment internally.

Boundary Rule:
Table A is metaphor only. No internal logic is ever described.

2. The Hallway — Neutral Passage of Time

Classification: Narrative IP
Definition:
A period requiring no new decisions or analysis.
Represents the time-based maturation of clarity after a Check is created.

Boundary Rule:
The Hallway describes waiting, not computation.

3. Table B — The Settlement Desk

Classification: Narrative IP
Definition:
The phase where the Founder redeems a matured Sentinel Check by converting it into an Order Sheet and submitting it externally.

Boundary Rule:
Table B concerns execution, not detection.

4. Shadow Momentum™

Classification: Protected IP (Trademark)
Definition:
A proprietary narrative term describing the pre-expressed causal pressure field that forms before price becomes visible.

Boundary Rule:
Only the conceptual identity is used publicly. Mechanisms remain sealed.

5. Sentinel Check

Classification: Internal Proprietary IP
Definition:
The internal business instrument generated at Table A.
Represents adjudicated clarity derived from the Founder’s sealed workflow.

Boundary Rule:
The Check is never described, transmitted, or displayed outside the Founder workflow.

6. Founder Order Sheet

Classification: External Execution Artifact
Definition:
The only item allowed to exit the Firewall.
Contains position size, price, and wide-stop parameters submitted to external broker/API.

Boundary Rule:
Only the Order Sheet, not the Check, leaves the system.

7. Pressure Flip States

Classification: Narrative IP
Definition:
A high-level conceptual lens describing directional pressure alignment:

SELL → NO TRADE → BUY


These states are narrative representations of coherence transitions.

Boundary Rule:
These states must never be tied to internal logic, TE, kernels, or computational thresholds.

8. S³ (Sentinel Signal System)

Classification: Proprietary IP (Name Only)
Definition:
The brand name of Sentinel’s sealed computational engine.
The term refers to the existence of the engine, not its mechanics.

Boundary Rule:
S³ is referenced only by name, never by describing:

its inputs

its outputs

its transforms

its adjudication logic

any internal state

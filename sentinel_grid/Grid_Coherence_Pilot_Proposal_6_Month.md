# Sentinel Grid — 6-Month Coherence Pilot Proposal
**Status**: Ready for utility distribution  
**Prepared for**: [Utility Name]  
**Date**: November 23, 2025  
**Classification**: Public-safe, non-enabling

## Executive Summary
Sentinel Labs proposes a 6-month, low-risk pilot to demonstrate measurable coherence-based load-balancing and stability gains on a single distribution feeder or substation zone.  
All technology remains black-box; only externally observable grid metrics are shared.

## Pilot Objectives
| Objective | Target Metric (illustrative) | Measurement Method |
|---------|-----------------------------|---------------------|
| Reduce unserved energy | ≥5 % improvement | SCADA comparison pre/post |
| Lower frequency deviation events | ≥15 % reduction | Phasor measurement units |
| Defer capacitor bank switching | ≥10 % fewer operations | Existing capacitor controls |
| Demonstrate human-in-the-loop override | 100 % successful kill-switch tests | Monthly tabletop + live test |

## Scope of Work
| Phase | Duration | Activities |
|-------|----------|------------|
| 1 – Planning & Integration | Weeks 1–4 | Site survey, data-sharing agreement, kill-switch integration |
| 2 – Baseline Measurement | Weeks 5–8 | 4-week pre-deployment data collection |
| 3 – Coherence Deployment | Weeks 9–28 | Live coherence injection (black-box appliance) |
| 4 – Evaluation & Reporting | Weeks 29–32 | Independent third-party verification, final report |

## Sentinel Responsibilities
- Provide and install coherence appliance (tamper-evident, black-box)  
- 24×7 remote monitoring (no grid control)  
- Weekly performance summaries  
- Full emergency kill-switch under utility control  
- Final third-party verified report

## Utility Responsibilities
- Provide secure 480 V feed and fibre/comms point  
- Grant read-only SCADA/PMU access  
- Participate in monthly review calls  
- Execute kill-switch tests on request

## Commercial Terms (illustrative)
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Pilot fee | $0 (success-based) |
| Success milestone payment (upon ≥5 % unserved-energy reduction, verified) | $250,000 |
| Option to expand to full feeder / substation (24-month) | Separate MSA |

## Risk Mitigation & Exit Rights
- Utility may terminate pilot with 48 hours notice — no penalty  
- All equipment removed within 14 days of termination  
- Data ownership remains with utility

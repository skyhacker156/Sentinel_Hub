# Sentinel Bio — Deep-Sea Hydrothermal Vent Localization Pilot
**Status**: Ready for institutional distribution  
**Prepared for**: Oceanographic institutes, submersible operators, environmental agencies

**Classification**: Public-safe, non-enabling

## Executive Summary
Sentinel Labs proposes a 12-month research collaboration to demonstrate passive, ship-mounted coherence monitoring as a new modality for hydrothermal vent discovery and real-time characterisation.

## Scientific Rationale (public-domain)
Hydrothermal vents create persistent, kilometre-scale electron-density anomalies in the water column. Existing discovery methods rely on:
- Plume chemistry (days–weeks to map)
- Acoustic backscatter (metre-scale resolution)
- Magnetometry (limited depth range)

Coherence monitoring offers a complementary, wide-area, real-time signal that appears hours to days before conventional tracers become detectable.

## Pilot Objectives
| Objective                         | Target Outcome                              |
